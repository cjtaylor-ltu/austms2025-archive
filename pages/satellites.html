A day of ... Analysis & Geometry

This is a one day conference centred on analysis, geometry and their intersection. The aim of this meeting is to bring together participants with an interest in analysis and geometry from within Australia. This event is open to everyone and in particular, Honours students, PhD students and Postdocs are welcome and invited to attend the event.
